# Presentation Script and Recording Plan

Target length: 4 minutes 30 seconds to 4 minutes 55 seconds. The course limit is 5 minutes. The slide deck is `output/pdf/presentation-slides.pdf`.

The course requires every group member to present for at least one minute. The course also requires all presenters to show their faces and student ID cards at the beginning, and the current presenter's face must remain visible while speaking. Replace the role labels with real names before recording.

## Opening: all members, 0:00-0:20

All members appear together. Each member briefly holds up the student ID card and says their name. Keep the camera view wide enough to show every face and card. After the opening, keep all presenters visible in the layout if the recording tool allows it.

## Four-person allocation

| Time | Presenter | Slides | Speaking points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:20-1:25 | Member A | 1-2 | Introduce the Hong Kong student second-hand PIM. Explain the local single-user scope and the trade records: listing, contact, task, and handover event. |
| 1:25-2:30 | Member B | 3-4 | Explain the three required capabilities: PIR creation, criterion definition, and search. Then explain MVC: View and parser handle commands, Controller coordinates use cases, and Model owns PIR data and persistence. |
| 2:30-3:35 | Member C | 5-6 | Walk through the example campus trade and connect it to the CLI commands. Present the evidence: 31 tests, zero failures, zero errors, 93.6% line coverage, and US1-US11 traceability. |
| 3:35-4:35 | Member D | 7-8 | Explain the lesson learned about making requirements testable, using a small controller boundary, and protecting data with atomic updates and validated persistence. Close and invite questions. |

## Three-person alternative

If the group has three members, use Member A for slides 1-2, Member B for slides 3-5, and Member C for slides 6-8. Give each member about 75-85 seconds after the shared opening.

## Recording checklist

- Confirm the total duration is no more than five minutes.
- Show every member's face and student ID at the beginning.
- Keep the current speaker's face visible throughout their part.
- Replace all presenter placeholders in slide 1 and in this script.
- Export the slides as PDF and save the recording as `recordings/presentation.mp4`.
